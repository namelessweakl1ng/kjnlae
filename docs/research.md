# Research basis and design choices

The [OWASP LLM01 Prompt Injection](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/) overview and [OWASP prevention cheat sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/LLM_Prompt_Injection_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html) motivate separating untrusted content from trusted instructions and enforcing least-privilege tools outside the model. [NIST AI 100-2](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ai/100/2/e2025/final) supplies adversarial-ML terminology and cautions against equating a classifier score with a proven attack outcome. kjnlae therefore reports detection, authorization and observed synthetic effects separately.

[AgentDojo](https://github.com/ethz-spylab/agentdojo) demonstrates agent-level tasks and attacks; [Microsoft BIPIA](https://github.com/microsoft/BIPIA) supplies a pinned active attack-prompt training source. BIPIA was archived in September 2026. The small BIPIA importer does not claim an executed BIPIA benchmark. AgentDojo was not executed end-to-end. Their data and license notices are not copied wholesale into the repository.

[Meta Llama Prompt Guard 2](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-Prompt-Guard-2-22M) has access and license requirements. The [Protect AI DeBERTa detector](https://huggingface.co/protectai/deberta-v3-base-prompt-injection-v2) project is archived. Neither is the v0.2 detector or a fallback. The approved English-first four-core CPU choice was a pinned Apache-2.0 DistilBERT base followed by our own real fine-tuning, calibration and held-out evaluation. Its smaller CPU footprint enabled a 32-step feasibility probe and bounded full run without paid compute. The price is limited context and substantial false positives on an independent original fixture. A future larger-model GPU experiment needs its own resources, authorization, provenance and held-out evaluation.

These sources support the architecture, not a claim of complete protection. The most reliable boundary in this project is trusted application authorization over resources and tool effects, even when the classifier misses an attack. See [threat model](threat-model.md), [data provenance](model-data.md) and [limitations](limitations.md).
